What I learned

The honest three: where it hurt, what clicked, and the moment something changed.

😵 Friction

Determining what to feed the model is important. Quality of input will drive qualtiy of output. Hence sampling the imputs are so important.

🧠 Learning

There is more to AI Product Management than Vibe Coding. When the capability to build ("How") is democratized, the "What" and "Why" to build becomes even more important

💡 Aha

Building a product using a foundational model is one thing. Building a AI product from scratch is a whole different game all together.
